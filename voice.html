CHAPEL II

THE VOICE

A trigram chain over her half of the record and nothing else. It does not retrieve — every line below is new, and none of them were ever sent. Each pair of words hands off to a word that really did follow it, so the sentences are false and the grammar of them is entirely hers.

WHAT THIS IS. A word-level trigram model. Two words index every word that ever followed them in her messages; one is drawn at random, and the window slides. Utterances end where her real messages ended, because the terminator is part of the table.
WHAT IT IS NOT. Not her, not a quote, not a prediction, and not an opinion about her. It has no sentiment model, no curated vocabulary, no tuned parameters — there is nothing in it to have been tuned. It is the shape of 434,795 words with the meaning taken out.
