THE TEMPLE

ONE SUBJECT · NO EDITORIAL · BY THE DATA

Instruments built on Annie's half of the record — 68,998 messages, 434,795 words, eleven years — and on nothing else. They are not arguments and they do not have conclusions. Each one takes the counts and does exactly one strange thing with them.

THE RULE, WHICH IS THE WHOLE POINT.
No sentiment scoring. No curated vocabulary. No flagged phrases. No highlighted dates. No threshold chosen because of what it would surface. Every number these chapels display is a count, a timestamp, or a transition probability, computed over the whole of her half of the record with no message excluded.

That constraint is not modesty. Anything editorial would make the output a portrait of an argument. Left alone, the counts make a portrait of a person — and the interesting part is that nobody chose any of it. The one filter that exists is a closed-class stopword list used by the lexicon, published as LVAnnie.STOP so it can be read and disagreed with.
CHAPEL I

THE ORACLE

Ask it something and it answers with passages the record already contained. It generates nothing — every word it returns was written by someone on a real date. The draw is seeded by your question, so the same question returns the same answer forever. An oracle you can re-roll is a slot machine; one that never changes its mind is making a claim.

CHAPEL II

THE VOICE

A trigram chain over her messages alone. It does not retrieve: every line it produces is new, and none of them were ever sent. Each pair of words hands off to a word that really did follow it, so the sentences are false and the grammar of them is entirely hers. Turn it on and it does not stop talking.

CHAPEL III

THE CLOCK

Every message she ever sent, placed by the moment she sent it. Angle is the hour, radius is the date — first message at the centre, last at the rim. Nothing excluded, nothing smoothed. What the spiral draws is when she was awake, for eleven years. Hover any dot and read what it was.
