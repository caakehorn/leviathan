A THIRD SECTION · SIX INSTRUMENTS OVER THE QUESTION OF WHOSE DECISION IT WAS · NO PASSPHRASE, BECAUSE EVERY LINE HERE IS ALREADY PUBLIC

What this page is for, and what it does not claim

One question, asked narrowly: was Annie Ulmer's acquisition and use of controlled substances something she did, or something that was done to her? The six instruments here answer it out of the message record alone. Each pen is a count of cited lines, and every line it counts is readable verbatim beside it — click any of them and the file, the timestamp, the direction tag and the wiki page that carries it open in full.

WHAT THE RECORD SHOWS

  • She ran a supplier relationship of her own in 2018–2019 — travelling to the source, placing orders by size, moving money, absorbing losses when she was beaten, chasing the line when it went dead, and introducing other buyers to it. That is 2,010 days before the window in which she is said to have been made dependent.
  • Third parties routed their own requests through her. That is a supply node, not a victim of one.
  • In the terminal window she initiates. The clearest instance is March 11, 2026: she opens the re-entry with the ask, offers an errand as the consideration, and writes the refusal into the message herself — "You can tell me no."
  • The record contains no instance of supply withheld as leverage or punishment, across the entire terminal window, including hours after the worst thing Dan ever said to her and minutes after the confession he had spent months trying to obtain. Coercive control runs on the threat of withdrawal. There is no withdrawal here to threaten with.
  • For every month of the relationship except the fifteen shaded in CLOCK, she had her own income, her own route to supply, or both. That window begins with a move she made and a funded apartment she declined, and it ends with a job she took four weeks before the relationship closed.
  • The most independent witness in the corpus is the man she left Dan for — who had attacked Dan publicly two weeks earlier, and who then described the addiction as hers, unprompted, to the last person with any claim on his loyalty.

THE ASK — ANNIE’S OWN WORDS, FROM THE RAW LOGS

This instrument does not quote the wiki's summarizing prose. Every Annie-thread text export in wiki-brain's raw/self/message-csv/ was merged, the one UTC-stamped export converted to local time (it had been minting phantom duplicates four hours off), the result deduplicated, and Annie's side kept: 18,946 messages from Annie, 2025-02-01 to 2026-06-05, every month covered — no narrative gaps to explain away.

Classification is frame-and-object, not keyword: a message counts as a procurement ask only if it is a speech act aimed at somebody and names what it is for. Then every single hit was read by hand and the false positives struck. The precision that survived that audit is printed on the instrument, because a number nobody can check is not evidence.

The shape that came out is not the one the summaries imply. Of 357 procurement messages from Annie:

  • 42 are Annie naming the size of the buy — the denominated order. Dan asks, Annie answers with a number. "How much?" → "60." "I need a number now / she's making the call" → "100."
  • 131 are Annie asking for it — mostly oblique, in the thread's own euphemisms: "get one," "get something," "I want a line," "More z," "Why can't I just get it tonight," "I want to get it first. I am dying here."
  • 137 are Annie raising the money, including the ten times Annie asks Annie's own mother or sister for it.
  • 25 are Annie handing Dan the cardless code to Annie's own bank account — "I'll send you an access code. Take fifty out." "Can you please go to atm for me. Get out $80. I will send you the card less code."
  • 22 are Annie asking whether the source is reachable — "Is there any possibility of seeing Bop," "Did you ever end up seeing Bop," "Is he coming."

Two findings a keyword sweep cannot reach. The denominated order is one: the message is just 60, and it reads as noise until Dan's side is put back beside it, at which point it reads as a purchase order. The last one is "100?", sent at 5:51 PM on May 31, 2026 — six hours before "Goodbye forever."

The cardless code is the other, and it inverts the dependency picture outright. Twenty-five times Annie sends Dan a one-hour access code to Annie's own bank account so Dan can withdraw the purchase money at an ATM. A person being supplied by someone does not hand that person banking credentials to fund the purchase.

And on March 6, 2026 Annie names the alternative herself: "I'll find someone else to get it from and you'll never have to deal with this again." That sentence is not available to somebody whose access runs through Dan alone.

Annie states 68 dollar amounts totalling $2,629 and announces 32 ATM runs. The transaction the record actually documents is a purchase Annie funds and directs, not a supply Annie is handed. Annie's own line, March 26 2025: "When I am the one who will be fucking paying for it."

The rate is real too, and it is the corpus's own: a base-rate control run on 2026-07-18 found 97.2% of a random sample of all Annie's messages in the Aug 2025 – Mar 2026 window had a request within ±24h. That control was built to test whether Annie's love-declarations were procurement signals; it killed that claim and established the ask frequency in the same pass.

The number worth setting against all of it: across the same 222-day window Annie made seven unprompted enquiries after how Dan was — four once the perfunctory ones are netted out. Once every fifty-five days.

WHAT THIS PAGE DOES NOT CLAIM

It does not claim Dan is a bystander. The same corpus states plainly that he was the supply-chain architect of the terminal phase, that structuring a dependency was his doing, that his verbal abuse quadrupled across seven months and reproduces exactly from raw, and that the surveillance arc points toward control. Those findings are on this page, in the same lanes as everything else, under AGAINST DAN in PROVENANCE. Structuring a supply relationship with someone who was already using for a decade is not the same act as creating the use — but it is not nothing, and nothing here pretends otherwise.

It also does not use the record's most quotable statistic. The 187:4 love-to-request ratio, long cited as proof that her affection was a procurement instrument, was killed by a base-rate control the corpus ran on itself on 2026-07-18 and inverted by the directional test. It is absent from every lane here except as an example of the record correcting against its own author.

SOURCING
