What this page is for, and what it does not claim

One question, asked narrowly: was Annie Ulmer's acquisition and use of controlled substances something she did, or something that was done to her? The six instruments here answer it out of the message record alone. Each pen is a count of cited lines, and every line it counts is readable verbatim beside it — click any of them and the file, the timestamp, the direction tag and the wiki page that carries it open in full.

WHAT THE RECORD SHOWS

  • She ran a supplier relationship of her own in 2018–2019 — travelling to the source, placing orders by size, moving money, absorbing losses when she was beaten, chasing the line when it went dead, and introducing other buyers to it. That is 2,010 days before the window in which she is said to have been made dependent.
  • Third parties routed their own requests through her. That is a supply node, not a victim of one.
  • In the terminal window she initiates. The clearest instance is March 11, 2026: she opens the re-entry with the ask, offers an errand as the consideration, and writes the refusal into the message herself — "You can tell me no."
  • The record contains no instance of supply withheld as leverage or punishment, across the entire terminal window, including hours after the worst thing Dan ever said to her and minutes after the confession he had spent months trying to obtain. Coercive control runs on the threat of withdrawal. There is no withdrawal here to threaten with.
  • For every month of the relationship except the fifteen shaded in CLOCK, she had her own income, her own route to supply, or both. That window begins with a move she made and a funded apartment she declined, and it ends with a job she took four weeks before the relationship closed.
  • The most independent witness in the corpus is the man she left Dan for — who had attacked Dan publicly two weeks earlier, and who then described the addiction as hers, unprompted, to the last person with any claim on his loyalty.

THE ASK LEDGER, AND WHY IT HAS TWO NUMBERS

THE ASK plots every request from her the record dates, from February 2025 forward, on a real calendar with a running counter underneath. Two numbers come out of it and they must not be confused.

  • Thirteen itemised asks — the ones the wiki dates and describes. That number is small because the corpus narrates only 13% of the 500 days closely, not because those were the only asks. The lit bands on the timeline are the narrated stretches; the dark ones are missing record.
  • Near-daily — the actual rate, and it came out of a control run against Dan's own favourite statistic. Testing whether her love-declarations were procurement signals, the 2026-07-18 recount found 97.2% of a random sample of all her messages in the Aug 2025 – Mar 2026 window had a request within ±24h. That killed the love claim and established the ask frequency in the same pass. No integer total of her requests exists anywhere in the corpus, and this page does not invent one.

The number worth setting against both: across the same 222-day window she made seven unprompted enquiries after how he was — four once the perfunctory ones are netted out. Once every fifty-five days.

WHAT THIS PAGE DOES NOT CLAIM

It does not claim Dan is a bystander. The same corpus states plainly that he was the supply-chain architect of the terminal phase, that structuring a dependency was his doing, that his verbal abuse quadrupled across seven months and reproduces exactly from raw, and that the surveillance arc points toward control. Those findings are on this page, in the same lanes as everything else, under AGAINST DAN in PROVENANCE. Structuring a supply relationship with someone who was already using for a decade is not the same act as creating the use — but it is not nothing, and nothing here pretends otherwise.

It also does not use the record's most quotable statistic. The 187:4 love-to-request ratio, long cited as proof that her affection was a procurement instrument, was killed by a base-rate control the corpus ran on itself on 2026-07-18 and inverted by the directional test. It is absent from every lane here except as an example of the record correcting against its own author.

SOURCING

Every record is drawn from the public wiki at github.com/caakehorn/wiki-brain, shipped unencrypted in this repository as data/wiki-data.json. Records carry the wiki's own provenance tiers, and where the wiki flags a line as dossier-only, undated, contradicted or unresolved, the flag travels with it and is shown in the source card. PROVENANCE sorts the whole case by that tier so the weak sourcing is impossible to hide behind the strong.
