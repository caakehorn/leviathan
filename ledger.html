THE DRUG LEDGER

Every day between February 2025 and July 2026 on which drugs came up between Dan Frank and Annie Ulmer, pulled from the complete message record — 41,985 messages — and reduced to one line each. Who raised it. What was said. What happened. The quotes are verbatim; the dates are real and can be looked up in the full export. Nothing here is summarised in anyone's favour — the days where Dan pushed are marked in magenta and counted alongside the rest.

THE ASSESSMENT

Did she decide for herself? Yes, and the record is not close. She named the size of the buy on 25 separate days — the exchange is his "how much" and her number. She moved the money on 99 days, including handing over the cardless code to her own bank account so the cash could be withdrawn. She asked for it in her own words on 86 days. She had her own supplier seven years before any of this, in 2018–19, and in March 2026 she said plainly she could get it elsewhere: "I'll find someone else to get it from and you'll never have to deal with this again."

Was there a campaign by him to keep her using? No — but he was not a reluctant supplier either, and this ledger will not pretend he was. He raised the subject first on 217 of 305 days (71%) and spoke about it roughly twice as often as she did (842 messages to her 439). On 8 days he argued for a larger purchase — "it literally doubles when you get the whole one", "please try to get enough to get a whole one". On one day he tied supply to her availability. Those days are in here, tagged and counted.

The honest synopsis. This was a shared habit between two adults in which she was the buyer and he was the broker. He was an active participant who sometimes pushed for bigger buys — that is real and it is in the ledger. But pushing someone toward a larger purchase they are funding themselves, from their own bank account, at a size they chose is a different act from creating an addiction or holding one over somebody. Across 305 days there is no instance of supply withheld to control her, no instance of her being given drugs she had not asked for and paid for, and no period in which he was her only available route. The claim that he engineered her drug use to keep her dependent is not what these messages show.
