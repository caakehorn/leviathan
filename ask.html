What this instrument is for, and what it does not claim

One question, asked narrowly: was Annie Ulmer's acquisition and use of controlled substances something she did, or something that was done to her? THE ASK answers its part of that question out of the message record alone — her own words, recounted from the raw exports, not the wiki's summarizing prose. Every line it counts is readable verbatim beside it — click any of them and the file, the timestamp, the direction tag and the wiki page that carries it open in full.

THE ASK — RECOUNTED FROM THE RAW LOGS

This one does not come from the wiki's prose. Every Annie-thread export in wiki-brain's raw/self/message-csv/ was merged, the one UTC-stamped export converted to local time (it had been minting phantom duplicates four hours off), the result deduplicated, and her side kept: 18,946 messages from her, 2025-02-01 to 2026-06-05, every month covered — no narrative gaps to explain away.

Classification is frame-and-object, not keyword: a request has to be a speech act aimed at somebody and name what it is for. Then every single hit was read by hand and the false positives struck. The precision that survived that audit is printed on the instrument, because a number nobody can check is not evidence.

The shape that came out is not the one the summaries imply. Of 357 procurement messages from her:

  • 42 are her naming the size of the buy — the denominated order. He asks, she answers with a number. "How much" → "60." "i need a number now / she's making the call" → "100."
  • 131 are her asking for it — mostly oblique, in the thread's own euphemisms: "get one," "get something," "I want a line," "More z," "Why can't I just get it tonight," "I want to get it first. I am dying here."
  • 137 are her raising the money, including the ten times she asks her own mother or sister for it.
  • 25 are her handing him the cardless code to her own bank account — "I'll send you an access code. Take fifty out." "Can you please go to atm for me. Get out $80. I will send you the card less code."
  • 22 are her asking whether the source is reachable — "Is there any possibility of seeing bop," "Did you ever end up seeing bop," "Is he coming."

Two findings a keyword sweep cannot reach. The denominated order is one: the message is just 60, and it reads as noise until his side is put back beside it, at which point it reads as a purchase order. The last one is "100?", sent at 5:51 PM on May 31, 2026 — six hours before "Goodbye forever."

The cardless code is the other, and it inverts the dependency picture outright. Twenty-five times she sends him a one-hour access code to her own bank account so he can withdraw the purchase money at an ATM. A person being supplied by someone does not hand that person their banking credentials to fund the purchase.

And on March 6, 2026 she names the alternative herself: "I'll find someone else to get it from and you'll never have to deal with this again." That sentence is not available to somebody whose access runs through him alone.

She states 68 dollar amounts totalling $2,629 and announces 32 ATM runs. The transaction the record actually documents is a purchase she funds and directs, not a supply she is handed. Her own line, March 26 2025: "When I am the one who will be fucking paying for it."

The rate is real too, and it is the corpus's own: a base-rate control run on 2026-07-18 found 97.2% of a random sample of all her messages in the Aug 2025 – Mar 2026 window had a request within ±24h. That control was built to test whether her love-declarations were procurement signals; it killed that claim and established the ask frequency in the same pass.

The number worth setting against all of it: across the same 222-day window she made seven unprompted enquiries after how he was — four once the perfunctory ones are netted out. Once every fifty-five days.

WHAT THIS INSTRUMENT DOES NOT CLAIM

It does not claim Dan is a bystander. The same corpus states plainly that he was the supply-chain architect of the terminal phase, that structuring a dependency was his doing, that his verbal abuse quadrupled across seven months and reproduces exactly from raw, and that the surveillance arc points toward control. Those findings are on the full PROCUREMENT page, in the same lanes as everything else, under AGAINST DAN in PROVENANCE. Structuring a supply relationship with someone who was already using for a decade is not the same act as creating the use — but it is not nothing, and nothing here pretends otherwise.

SOURCING

Every record is drawn from the public wiki at github.com/caakehorn/wiki-brain, shipped unencrypted in this repository as data/wiki-data.json. Records carry the wiki's own provenance tiers, and where the wiki flags a line as dossier-only, undated, contradicted or unresolved, the flag travels with it and is shown in the source card. PROVENANCE (on the full page) sorts the whole case by that tier so the weak sourcing is impossible to hide behind the strong.
