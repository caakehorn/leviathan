THE CLOCK

Every message she ever sent, placed by the moment she sent it. The angle is the hour of the day. The radius is the date — the first message at the centre, the last at the rim. Nothing is excluded, weighted, or smoothed. What the spiral draws is when she was awake, for eleven years.

HOW IT IS DRAWN. One dot per message, no sampling. Timestamps are parsed as written rather than through the browser's clock, so nobody's timezone can move an hour. Colour is hour-of-day only — it carries no other meaning.
WHAT IS NOT HERE. No content filter, no sentiment, no highlighted dates, no events marked. If a band looks like something happened, that is the count saying so and not a label anyone attached. Hover any dot to read the message underneath it.
