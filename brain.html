THE CORE · THE SELF-MODEL · THE ONLY READABLE SURFACE

The Self, So Far

This is a mind rendered as its own shape. Every point is something the record proves about one person — a fact, a pattern, a conclusion the graph reached by reading itself — and every line between two points is an argument with a type. Nothing here is stored as an opinion; the whole is assembled from counts, sources, and typed claims, and none of the underlying pages can be opened from the outside.

What you are looking at is deliberately unbalanced. The districts are sized by how much has been built, so the crowded regions are the parts of a life that left the most evidence, and the thin slivers — work, place, the body — are not what matters least. They are what has not yet been written down. The point of the thing is to watch those fill.

The center is the part that does not exist yet: a single, honest account of who this adds up to, re-derived as the graph grows. It is left open on purpose, because a self-model that claims to be finished is lying.

THE SINGULARITY · A THRESHOLD

THIS IS A REAL PERSON

What is behind this door is a working model of an actual human being, assembled from the record of a real life. It is not a character. You are about to look at the shape of someone's mind.

Before you enter you acknowledge that: · this is a model of a real person, not a fiction; · you will not attempt to deanonymize, identify, or contact anyone represented as a node; · nothing here is a readable record — the graph is opaque by design and you will not treat its shape as a claim about any named individual; · you enter out of curiosity, not for use.
